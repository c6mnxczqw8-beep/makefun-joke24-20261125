# Office joke candidates (draft pass)

Drafted across different office-comedy mechanisms, then scored
against the judge's criteria: really an office joke, punchline
surprises and pays off, recognisable to anyone who has sat in an
office, tight single line.

1. Escalation: "This meeting should have been an email, and the
   email should have been a text nobody sent." -- strong premise
   (the most universal office complaint there is), but the ending
   stops one step early; the ladder wants a third rung.
2. Printer personification: "The printer has been 'almost out of
   toner' for six months. It is the only one here honest about
   doing the bare minimum to survive." -- funny, but two sentences
   and softer payoff.
3. Calendar as ally: "My calendar says I am in a meeting until
   five. My calendar is the only coworker who protects my time."
   -- warm rather than laugh-out-loud.
4. Status theatre: "I love my job. The job is a group project
   where I am the group." -- tight, but the group-project frame is
   well worn online.
5. Reply-all dread: "Nothing unites an office like watching
   someone hit reply-all and typing 'please stop replying all'
   to all." -- recognisable, slightly tangled syntax.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass that gave the
ladder its third rung. The draft ended at "a text nobody sent";
the shipped line continues "and the text should have been a
thought nobody had", which is where the laugh lands -- the
message shrinks all the way down to a thought that never even
happened. 126 characters.
